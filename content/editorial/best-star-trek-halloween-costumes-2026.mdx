## How to think about it
Start by identifying which Star Trek era resonates most with you—Original Series, The Next Generation, or Discovery. Match the costume’s level of detail to your event: a simple shirt and badge for a casual party, or a full uniform with phaser for a convention. Consider buying a size that accommodates growth if you’re purchasing for a child, and keep accessories in a dedicated bag for future reuse.
