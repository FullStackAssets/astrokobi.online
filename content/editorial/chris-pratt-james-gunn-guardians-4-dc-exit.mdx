When Marvel’s James Gunn announced his departure from DC Studios, the news sparked a flurry of speculation about his next move. Chris Pratt, who has starred as Star‑Lord for six MCU outings, said he would love to reunite with Gunn for a fourth Guardians of the Galaxy film. The actor’s comment arrived just hours after the DC shake‑up was confirmed, linking two of Hollywood’s biggest franchise dramas. If Gunn returns to the MCU, the Guardians saga could extend beyond the teased post‑credits hook in Vol. 3. The possibility reshapes the strategic landscape for both Marvel and the newly restructured DC.

<Callout type="takeaway">Gunn’s exit from DC could open the door for a Guardians IV under his direction.</Callout>

## What happened
James Gunn and co‑chief Peter Safran were removed from DC Studios on Nov. 9 after Skydance’s merger, with Jeremy Latcham named president and chief creative officer. The leadership change has left the DC slate in flux and prompted industry observers to track Gunn’s next project.

In an interview with Moviezine, Chris Pratt said, “That would be awesome. I love working with James… if he wanted to come back and bring it back, I would love that.” Pratt noted he has not spoken with Gunn since the news broke, but expressed enthusiasm for a potential Guardians IV.

## Why it matters
Gunn’s departure removes a proven franchise architect from DC, potentially weakening its upcoming slate while freeing him to re‑engage with the MCU. A fourth Guardians film would extend Marvel’s cosmic narrative, satisfy fan demand hinted at by the Vol. 3 end‑credits, and could influence the pacing of Phase 6 releases. Studios, investors, and audiences alike are watching to see whether the creative chemistry that drove the first three films can be recaptured.

Treat Pratt’s comment as an indicator of interest, not a formal greenlight. For fans, consider the narrative threads left open in Vol. 3 and evaluate how a fourth installment could logically expand the cosmic arc without retreading familiar ground.
