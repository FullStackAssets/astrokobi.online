## What happened

The monster’s cinematic lore traces back to its first appearance in the 1964 film Ghidorah, the Three‑Headed Monster, where it devastated Venus before crashing onto Earth.

Separately, the astronomical community named a massive galaxy aggregation the King Ghidorah Supercluster after the kaiju’s fame. This supercluster ranks among the largest known structures, spanning hundreds of millions of light‑years and containing thousands of galaxies bound by gravity.
