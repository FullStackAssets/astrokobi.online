## What happened
The fourth meeting between the programs sees Baylor leading the series 2‑1, having won the last two encounters, including a 30‑3 rout in Waco last season. This year’s uniform pays homage to the 1960s Mercury, Gemini and Apollo missions, reinforcing the Space Game tradition that began in 2017. Baylor entered the contest reeling from the dismissal of Dave Aranda, with interim head coach Joe Klanderman taking over. The Bears lead the Big 12 in quarterback sacks (27) and rank among the conference leaders in takeaways (13), while UCF has forced 12 turnovers this season. Both squads have demonstrated the ability to generate defensive disruption, setting the stage for a contest that could be decided by pressure and opportunism.
