## What happened
Home Alone returns to the spotlight as the top‑ranked holiday comedy on Disney+, offering Macaulay Culkin’s iconic Kevin McCallister and a series of booby‑trapped shenanigans that resonate strongly during the Christmas season. The streaming platform highlights the film’s continued cultural relevance, noting its #8 nationwide ranking in recent viewership metrics.

The second recommendation is a preview of the forthcoming Marvel blockbuster, colloquially dubbed Avengers: Doomsday, designed to prime audiences for the next phase of the MCU. Alongside the superhero teaser, Disney+ features a fantasy epic derived from a bestselling novel that has been adapted twice, delivering a high‑stakes adventure for fans of literary adaptations.

Although Monsters, Inc. remains the only Disney+ title trending nationally at the moment, it is deliberately omitted from this weekend’s curated list to provide variety beyond animation.

## Why it matters
By pairing a timeless family comedy with a Marvel teaser and a literary fantasy, Disney+ taps into distinct audience segments—families, superhero enthusiasts, and fantasy readers—maximizing subscriber retention during a competitive streaming period. The strategy also builds momentum for upcoming releases, turning weekend viewing into a promotional runway that can boost anticipation metrics and drive future sign‑ups.
