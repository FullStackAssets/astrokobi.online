The Denver Nuggets arrived back from a preseason tour in Mexico only to discover a significant flight delay that kept their charter grounded. The delay forced the team’s starting lineup to be listed as questionable for the upcoming matchup against the Houston Rockets. With limited practice time and disrupted travel routines, the uncertainty could affect the game’s competitiveness and the league’s scheduling.

## What happened
The charter flight that was supposed to return the Nuggets from Mexico experienced an unexpected delay due to airport congestion and weather advisories. Team officials reported that the aircraft could not depart at the scheduled time, leaving the players on the tarmac for an extended period.

When the flight finally left, the team arrived in Denver later than planned, and the coaching staff had to assess player readiness. As a result, the starting five—Jamal Murray, Nikola Jokić, Michael Porter Jr., Aaron Gordon, and Kentavious Caldwell‑Pope—were all listed as questionable for the game against the Rockets, pending medical evaluation.

## Why it matters
The uncertainty around the Nuggets’ starters impacts competitive balance, as the Rockets will prepare for a lineup that may not be at full strength. Fans and broadcasters also face scheduling challenges, with potential adjustments to start times and broadcast slots. Moreover, the incident highlights how travel logistics can influence on‑court performance and league operations.
