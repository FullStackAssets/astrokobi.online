Marvel has refreshed its 2019 blockbuster for the streaming era. 'Avengers: Endgame Encore' arrived on Disney+ on November 16, 2026, bundling four minutes of new bonus scenes and a teaser for the upcoming 'Avengers: Doomsday.' The release follows a limited theatrical re‑run that earned $86 million on September 25, 2026. By tying the extra content to the next MCU chapter, Disney+ is turning a nostalgic revisit into a promotional launchpad. For fans and casual viewers alike, the timing reshapes how the franchise builds anticipation.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Encore release uses added footage to prime audiences for the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday.</Callout>

## What happened
After a successful limited theater run earlier this fall, the re‑release earned $86 million and featured four bonus scenes totaling roughly four minutes, plus a special "Doomsday" preview. The theatrical event also reclaimed the all‑time box‑office peak by surpassing "Avatar."

The extended version was added to Disney+ on November 16, 2026, and is part of the platform’s "Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday" collection. Subscribers can watch it with the standard Disney+ plans ($11.99/month with ads or $18.99/month ad‑free). No separate purchase is required.

## Why it matters
The move illustrates how Disney leverages legacy content to generate buzz for upcoming releases, turning a nostalgic re‑watch into a marketing engine. By embedding the preview directly in a streaming update, the studio nudges both existing fans and new subscribers toward the December 18 debut of "Avengers: Doomsday." The strategy also reinforces Disney+ as a hub for exclusive MCU experiences, potentially boosting subscriber growth amid fierce streaming competition.

## How to think about it
Treat the Encore release as a supplemental briefing rather than a standalone viewing. If you haven’t seen the original "Endgame," watch it first to avoid confusion. Use the bonus scenes to spot narrative threads that may lead into "Doomsday," but keep expectations measured—four minutes won’t reveal the full plot. Evaluate whether the added value justifies upgrading to an ad‑free Disney+ plan, especially if you plan to follow the upcoming MCU releases.

<Callout type="warning">Avoid watching the bonus scenes if you intend to experience "Avengers: Doomsday" without any spoilers; the preview contains key plot hints.</Callout>
