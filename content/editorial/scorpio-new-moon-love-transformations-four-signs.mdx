## What happened
The Scorpio new moon arrives on November 8 (PST/EST) and is framed by several powerful aspects. Mars, Scorpio’s traditional ruler, sits in passionate Leo and entered a pre‑retrograde shadow on November 5, hinting that themes emerging now may resurface during its retrograde from January to April 2027. Venus is retrograde in Libra and Mercury retrograde in Scorpio, both stationing direct on November 13, making the window before that date more suited for reflection than decisive action.

On November 10, Venus sextiles Mars, offering a brief window to reconnect with desires and consider necessary changes in love or finances. Meanwhile, Pluto in Aquarius trines Uranus in Gemini, underscoring a larger theme of liberation and reinvention that may manifest through unexpected circumstances. The corresponding Scorpio full moon on April 20, 2027 will serve as a culmination point, potentially leaving individuals feeling like entirely new versions of themselves.
