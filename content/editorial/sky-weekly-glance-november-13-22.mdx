## How to think about it
Treat the weekly guide as a checklist: first identify the high‑priority targets (e.g., planets at opposition), then slot in secondary items like the moon phase or meteor shower. Match each target to the equipment you have—binoculars for bright planets, a small telescope for lunar detail, and a camera with a wide‑angle lens for meteors. Finally, factor in local conditions such as cloud cover and light pollution, adjusting your schedule accordingly.

<Callout type="warning">Do not attempt deep‑sky imaging on nights when the moon is near full; the bright sky will wash out faint objects.</Callout>
