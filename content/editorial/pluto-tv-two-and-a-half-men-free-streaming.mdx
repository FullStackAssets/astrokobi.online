Pluto TV announced that it will begin streaming the full run of the hit sitcom “Two and a Half Men” for free, joining four other Warner Bros. series in a new output agreement. Starting Nov 20, all 12 seasons (262 episodes) will be available alongside “Veronica Mars,” “The Jamie Foxx Show,” “Martin” and “The Wayans Bros.” The move marks the first time the sitcom is offered without a subscription fee. It reflects a broader push by ad‑supported video‑on‑demand (AVOD) platforms to capture legacy TV audiences.
<Callout type="takeaway">Pluto TV’s new Warner Bros. sitcom lineup makes free, ad‑supported streaming a mainstream destination for legacy TV hits.</Callout>
## What happened
Pluto TV’s senior VP of content partners, Will Gurman, confirmed that the network secured an output pact with Warner Bros. TV, delivering five sitcoms to its free service. “Two and a Half Men” will launch on Nov 20 with its complete 12‑season catalog, while “The Jamie Foxx Show” (5 seasons, 100 episodes) and “Martin” (5 seasons, 132 episodes) arrive on Nov 15. “Veronica Mars” (3 seasons, 64 episodes) and “The Wayans Bros.” (5 seasons, 101 episodes) follow a few days later.
The additions come as Pluto TV has added more than 25 library titles this year, driving a 70 % increase in its AVOD viewership. The platform also recently introduced 14 FAST channels featuring Warner Bros. content, expanding its linear‑plus‑on‑demand offering.
## Why it matters
By moving legacy sitcoms to a free, ad‑supported model, Pluto TV taps a demographic that has largely migrated away from subscription services. The strategy boosts ad inventory, diversifies revenue, and pressures rivals to broaden their own AVOD catalogs. For advertisers, the curated library offers a predictable audience of nostalgia‑driven viewers; for consumers, it provides instant access without a paywall, reshaping expectations of how classic TV can be monetized.
